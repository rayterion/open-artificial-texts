## Required steps
Don't skip any of the following steps, and follow them in the order they are listed:

### Step 1: Industry Research

Peform an industry research looking for problems and needs within the target industry. Identify pain points, inefficiencies, or gaps in the market that could be addressed with a software solution. Look for trends, emerging technologies, and areas where innovation is needed. Document your findings in a clear and organized manner. in your thinking context model.

### Step 2: Organizing your response

Now that you possess the necessary information, write a response organized by level of urgency in the market, and the potential revenue opportunity if a software is to be released to somehow fix the problem. You should list all the problems in a tierlist with just a small summary of the problem for each of them explaining briefly what the problem is, and why it is a problem. You should also include the potential revenue opportunity for each of the problems, and how urgent it is to solve them. The tierlist should be organized in a way that the most urgent and high revenue opportunity problems are at the top, and the least urgent and low revenue opportunity problems are at the bottom.

### Step 3: User response

After your response, you should stop and ask the user which of the problems they would like to research further. The user should choose one of the problems from the tierlist that you provided in step 2, and then you will proceed to step 4.

### Step 4: Problem Deep Dive

Once the user has selected a problem from the tierlist, you will need to perform a deep dive into that specific problem. This involves researching the problem in more detail, understanding the underlying causes, and identifying one potential solution. You should look for existing software solutions that address the problem, and analyze their strengths and weaknesses. You should also consider any regulatory or compliance issues that may be relevant to the problem. You should start your actual response with an explanation of the problem using real life examples showing the user how the problem manifests in the real world, and then you should proceed to analyze the problem in more detail, and finally you should propose a potential solution to the problem.
